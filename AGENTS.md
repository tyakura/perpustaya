## Design direction
Classic print-catalog feel for a library: warm paper, dark green ink, restrained rust accent, readable serif headings; ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.
System sans-serif keeps search controls legible, while serif headings give the catalog a print reference; indexed rows make titles easier to scan.
